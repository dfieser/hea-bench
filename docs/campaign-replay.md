## The loop against random selection

Pool: the 384 descriptor-scorable Borg hardness alloys. The hardest is 1183 HV, and the ten hardest are at least 875 HV. Each of 25 runs starts from 10 random alloys and measures batches of 5 until 50 alloys are measured, once by the loop's expected-improvement ranking and once by random picks from the same start.

Runs that measured one of the ten hardest alloys within 50: 25 of 25 by the loop, 16 of 25 by random picks.

The loop's 90 percent intervals held the revealed hardness for 903 of its 1000 suggestions (0.903). The loop chooses suggestions for high predicted hardness and high model disagreement, which is where intervals are most likely to miss. The figure is therefore coverage under selection, the case a user meets.
